### 🧠 &nbsp; How I Think
<sub><code>↳ opinions formed by shipping, not reading</code></sub>

</div>

<br/>

<table border="0" cellspacing="0" cellpadding="20" width="100%">
<tr>

<td align="center" valign="top" width="25%">

**`01 / UNDERSTAND`**

The half-hour you spend  
mapping domain concepts  
saves three days of refactors.

*Model the problem.  
Name things right.  
Then open the IDE.*

</td>

<td align="center" valign="top" width="25%">

**`02 / ARCHITECT`**

Schema mistakes compound.  
Naming mistakes compound.  
Boundary mistakes compound.

*Good architecture  
isn't clever —  
it disappears.*

</td>

<td align="center" valign="top" width="25%">

**`03 / SHIP`**

Prod teaches things  
no local env can.

*v1 imperfect > v∞  
still in your head.*

Ship. Log. Measure.  
Then improve.

</td>

<td align="center" valign="top" width="25%">

**`04 / OWN IT`**

Frameworks are tools.  
You are the engineer.

*Read the source.  
Understand the tradeoffs.  
Know why, not just how.*

</td>
